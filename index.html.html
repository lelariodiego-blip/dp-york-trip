Insider Local Secrets

York's Hidden Snickelways & Secret Corners

Beyond the famous landmarks lie narrow medieval alleyways ("snickelways"), Roman cellars, and riverside delicacies curated by our local guides.

Medieval Alleyways

The Snickelways of York

Over 50 named medieval footpaths—including Mad Alice Lane and Lund's Court—weave between Stonegate and The Shambles like a real-life labyrinth.

Roman Subterranean

Treasurer's House Roman Cellar

Descend beneath a stately townhouse beside York Minster to stand on the original Roman road Via Decumana—site of York's most famous legionnaire sighting.

Golden Hour Walk

Bar Walls Sunrise Ramparts

Walk the elevated limestone section between Bootham Bar and Monk Bar at 8:00 AM for unobstructed, crowd-free views of York Minster's flying buttresses.

Yorkshire Gastronomy

Bettys Fat Rascal Scones

Order a warm "Fat Rascal" fruit scone with Yorkshire clotted cream at St. Helen's Square—a 1919 York tearoom institution.

Street Food Courtyard

Shambles Market Food Court

Tucked right behind the leaning Tudor shopfronts, this open-air market serves roast Yorkshire pudding wraps, artisan pies, and local fudge.

Feline Trail

The York Cat Trail

Spot over 20 sculpted cats perched on historic roofs and ledges across the city—originally placed centuries ago to ward off plague-carrying rats and evil spirits.

Our Story • Founded by Diego Lelario & Pierluigi Lovaglio

Two Founders, One Passion: Making York Unforgettable

DP Trip and Journey takes its name directly from its co-founders and York Travel Directors, Diego Lelario ("D") and Pierluigi Lovaglio ("P"). Frustrated by generic, rushed bus tours, Diego and Pierluigi designed a bespoke travel studio dedicated to immersive storytelling, walkable city cartography, and stress-free logistics for schools, families, and cultural travelers.
